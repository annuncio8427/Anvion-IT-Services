How ANVION Built the HOMELUXE Engine

Designing an enterprise luxury real estate marketplace requires seamless performance, multi-tiered security, dynamic Indian currency calculations, and certified regulatory workflows.

AI Valuations & INR Engine

Engineered real-time algorithmic valuations converting raw carpet area, micro-market capital values, and floor-rise indices into exact Crores (₹ Cr) and Lakhs (₹ L) calculations with historical appreciation graphs.

  • • Dynamic price-per-sq.ft benchmarking

  • • Certified wealth advisory lead routing

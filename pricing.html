Frequently Asked Questions

How does milestone-based payment work?

expand_more
We structure all engagements into transparent milestones (typically 40% Kickoff & Architecture, 30% Functional Staging Demo, and 30% Final Production Deployment & Handover). You review and approve each stage before release of milestone funds.

Do I own the full intellectual property and source code?

expand_more
Yes, unconditionally. Upon completion of milestone payments, 100% of the repository, source code, database schemas, Figma assets, and deployment keys belong entirely to you with zero licensing locks or recurring franchise fees.

Are there any recurring or hidden monthly fees?

expand_more
No. Our engineering contracts are fixed-cost or clearly defined sprint-based. Third-party hosting, domains, and API tokens (e.g. AWS, Vercel, Firebase, Razorpay) are billed directly to your own account so you have full financial sovereignty.

Can we start with an MVP and scale later?

expand_more
Absolutely. We build with modular, clean-architecture patterns (e.g., decoupled APIs, normalized relational databases, and reusable component libraries) specifically so your MVP can seamlessly expand into a high-scale enterprise platform.

Task Intelligence

Multi-Tier Priority & Due Date Sorting

Engineered to handle dense academic exam schedules (e.g. DTSP, DBMS, VLSI Design Practical & Theory) with granular urgency levels: High, Medium, and Low, coupled with instant search filtering.

  • tune Instant reactive filters: All, Active, Completed, Low, Medium, High
